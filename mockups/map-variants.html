Obscure Music Theory
1 Fade 2 Light cone 3 Orbit 4 Ridgeline 5 Spectrum

Five maps

The same 223 classes, the same colours and the same chosen class, drawn five ways. Choose a class in any map, or here, and all five follow. Only the map is mocked; the part below it stays as it is.

1

Fade

The map you have, with the thicket cut away. Only the lines one note away are drawn. Everything further is a dot that grows fainter the more notes it is from your class, and a soft wash goes from blue below to yellow above. Hover a lit dot to see the path back to your class.

2

Light cone

No lines at all. Your class is a point of light, and what it sits inside opens upward as a beam that fades as it climbs. What lies inside it narrows to a blue point below. The shape is how far its family reaches.

3

Orbit

The map bent into rings: one note at the centre, twelve at the rim. Subsets are inside your class's ring, in blue, and supersets outside it, in yellow, with a gradient that warms as it goes outward. Only direct neighbours get an arc.

4

Ridgeline

Each row of the map becomes a ridge, like stacked mountain ranges. Related classes push up peaks, with a gradient fill under them, and the nearer a class is, the higher its peak. Unrelated ones stay as a flat ripple.

5

Spectrum

Every row is a strip of small bars, like an audio meter. Related classes stand tall in colour and the rest lie flat, with a count at the end of each row (31 of 38). The quietest, most typographic option. Hover a bar to see its direct neighbours.

Tell me a number, or mix: for example the lines of 1 with the wash of 3.
